## Purpose

This Definition of Done checklist ensures every feature story delivered by the Commerce Platform team meets engineering quality, security, testing, and acceptance standards before being merged to main and released to production.

**Layer Legend:**
- **[L1-UNIVERSAL]** — Applies to ALL work items, no exceptions. Cannot be overridden without EM + CISO sign-off.
- **[L2-WORKTYPE]** — Applies to all Feature Stories. EM can grant one-sprint exemption with written justification.
- **[L3-TEAM]** — Team-specific based on Commerce Platform tech stack. Tech Lead approval required. Reviewed quarterly.
- **[L4-STORY]** — Story-specific criterion added by PO or Tech Lead during grooming. Must not contradict L1–L3.
