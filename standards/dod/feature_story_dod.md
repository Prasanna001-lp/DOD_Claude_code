## How to Use This Checklist

This Definition of Done applies to **all Feature Story work items** for the Commerce Platform team. Every criterion is **mandatory unless explicitly marked optional** or conditionally applicable.

**Gate Layers:**
- **[L1-UNIVERSAL]** — Cannot be overridden. Applies to all work items. EM + CISO sign-off to modify.
- **[L2-WORKTYPE]** — Applies to all Feature Stories. EM can grant one-sprint exemption with written justification.
- **[L3-TEAM]** — Team-specific additions. Tech Lead approval required. Reviewed quarterly.
- **[L4-STORY]** — Story-specific criteria added by PO or Tech Lead during grooming. Must not contradict L1–L3.

**Exception Policy:**
- Exemptions for [L2] and [L3] criteria require EM or Tech Lead written approval documented in the PR comment.
- [L1] criteria have **no exceptions**. Any violation blocks merge.
