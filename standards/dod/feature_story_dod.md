## Overview

This Definition of Done applies to **Feature Story** work items in the Commerce Platform team. All criteria must be verified before a feature story can be merged to the main branch and deployed to production.

**Legend:**
- **[L1-UNIVERSAL]** — Applies to ALL work items. Cannot be overridden. EM + CISO sign-off required to modify.
- **[L2-WORKTYPE]** — Applies to ALL feature stories. EM can grant one-sprint exemption with written justification.
- **[L3-TEAM]** — Commerce Platform team-specific criteria. Tech Lead approval required. Reviewed quarterly.
- **[L4-STORY]** — Story-specific criteria added by PO or Tech Lead during grooming. Optional section.
