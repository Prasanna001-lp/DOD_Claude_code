## Overview

This Definition of Done applies to **production release** work items. Every release must satisfy all [L1] universal criteria, all [L2] release-specific criteria, and all [L3] team-specific criteria before deployment to production.

**Layer Key:**
- **[L1-UNIVERSAL]** — Applies to ALL work items. Cannot be overridden. EM + CISO sign-off required to modify.
- **[L2-WORKTYPE]** — Applies to ALL releases. EM can grant one-sprint exemption with written justification.
- **[L3-TEAM]** — Commerce Platform team-specific. Tech Lead approval required. Reviewed quarterly.
- **[L4-STORY]** — Story-specific criteria added during release planning. Optional.

**Verification:** Email thread or Slack message to #customer-success channel shows notification sent with release summary at least 24 hours before deployment timestamp.  
**Exception:** Tech Lead can grant exemption for hotfix releases addressing P1 incidents (must notify CS immediately post-deploy).
