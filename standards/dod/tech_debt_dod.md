## Overview

This Definition of Done applies to all work items classified as **Tech Debt** in the Commerce Platform team backlog. Tech debt includes refactoring, code quality improvements, dependency upgrades, architectural simplifications, and technical infrastructure enhancements that do not deliver new user-facing features.

Every criterion below is **verifiable** (binary pass/fail), **traceable** to a source policy, and **tool-specific** to the team's tech stack (Java, Spring Boot, React, PostgreSQL, Kafka).

**Layer Legend:**
- **[L1-UNIVERSAL]** — Applies to ALL work items. Cannot be overridden. Requires EM + CISO sign-off to modify.
- **[L2-WORKTYPE]** — Applies to ALL tech debt items. EM can grant one-sprint exemption with written justification.
- **[L3-TEAM]** — Team-specific addition. Tech Lead approval required. Reviewed quarterly.
- **[L4-STORY]** — Story-specific criterion added during grooming. PO + Tech Lead agreement required.
